*Adventures in Fenestra* is a collection of modules for the BIND RPG.

# Playtested Modules

## The Goblin Hole

> They said it would be easy.  'Just a few goblins', they said, 'who stole the
> gold', they promised.  But goblins don't go to market to buy things with our
> coins.  And they don't fight, they just run away.
> 
> It's been half a day, and we've not seen a goblin since we came in - just the
> corpses of all the guards who came before us.  We're out of food and down to
> our last torch, and it's getting hard to breathe in this tunnel.  And we're
> so tired of crawling.  Half a mile of crawling through this narrow gap in the
> dark.

The module comes with complete rules at the front, pre-generated characters,
and a cave.  A horribly realistic cave.

# Untested Modules

## On the Trail of the Snail Lords

> I know an old lady who swallowed a fly...

Elves fix their problems with spells.

- No roads?  Make giant snails and use their trails.
- Giant snails eating all the food?  Crack the earth and live on raised garden
  plateaus.
- Can't get down from the plateau?  Just make vine bridges with magic.  Sorted!

Meanwhile, in the next elven settlement, giant snails are eating everything.
But the Lord of Oaths has a solution:

- Enchant the snails to eat local monsters (but what do the goblins eat?).
- Enchant the goblins to keep the local laws (they can raid food from the
  nearby elven settlement).
- Well-fed goblins grow until they turn into ogres, so best enchant the ogres
  to sleep.

As long as the goblins enforce the no-noise laws, the scattered sepulchres of
sleeping ogres should remain dormant.

[Download][feylands]

## The Fridge

> A morality tale about health and safety.

The player characters must descend into the town's giant fridge as it melts.
Various guilds request the following:

- Kill the hibernating monsters before they wake up, get out, and kill the
  citizens of the town.
- Stop the fridge melting and keep the monsters safely hibernating.
- Boat across the freezing lake of melted ice to save the three librarians
  (each of whom want room on the boat to save their books).
- Stop that thief escaping!

The player characters must deal with terrible foes, such as:

- Conflicting safety procedures.
- Not enough boats.
- Improperly stored larger, ready to burst.
- Barrels of flour everywhere.
- An open torch-flame in every hand.
- Hypoxia.
